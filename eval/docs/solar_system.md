# The Solar System

Jupiter is the largest planet in the Solar System. Its mass is more than twice that of all the other planets combined. The Great Red Spot is a giant storm on Jupiter that has lasted for at least 350 years.

Mars is often called the Red Planet because iron oxide on its surface gives it a reddish colour. Olympus Mons on Mars is the tallest volcano known in the Solar System, about 22 kilometres high.

Saturn is best known for its bright ring system, made mostly of ice particles with some rock and dust. Saturn's moon Titan has a thick nitrogen atmosphere and lakes of liquid methane.
